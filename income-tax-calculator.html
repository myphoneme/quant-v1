Frequently asked questions

Which regime is better for me?

It depends on how many deductions and exemptions you claim (like HRA, 80C, home loan interest, health insurance premium). Broadly, the New Regime tends to suit people with few deductions, while the Old Regime can work out better if you claim substantial 80C/80D/HRA benefits. Run both numbers above and compare.

Is the New Regime compulsory now?

The New Regime is the default regime for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27), but eligible taxpayers can still opt for the Old Regime when filing, subject to the applicable conditions and timelines.

Does this calculator account for HRA, 80C or other deductions in detail?

No — it's a simplified illustration using the standard deduction (for salaried/pensioners) and a single deductions figure you enter for the Old Regime. For an exact number, use the income tax department's official calculator or a tax professional, especially if your income has multiple components.

Is this tax advice from Phoneme Quant?

No. This is an educational, illustrative calculator only. Phoneme Quant is a distribution and referral business, not a tax advisor, and this tool does not account for every deduction, exemption or edge case in your specific situation.
