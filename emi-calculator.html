How EMI is calculated

Your Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) is calculated so that a fixed payment each month clears both principal and interest by the end of the tenure, using the standard reducing-balance formula. Early instalments are interest-heavy; later ones repay more principal, even though the EMI amount itself stays flat.

Two levers matter most for affordability: a longer tenure lowers the monthly EMI but increases total interest paid, while a shorter tenure raises the EMI but reduces the total cost of the loan.

Frequently asked questions

Does Phoneme Quant decide my interest rate or loan approval?

No. Loan enquiries are referred through Andromeda Sales and Distribution Private Limited, and the relevant bank or NBFC independently decides eligibility, interest rate, sanction and disbursal. This calculator uses the rate you enter, not a quoted offer.

Why is my EMI different from what a bank quoted me?

Banks price loans based on your credit score, income, existing obligations and the specific scheme, and may add processing fees or insurance that change the effective cost. Treat this calculator as a starting estimate, not a final figure.

What's the difference between flat rate and reducing balance rate?

Most personal, home and car loans in India use reducing balance interest, where interest is charged only on the outstanding principal each month — that's what this calculator uses. A flat rate charges interest on the full original amount throughout, which works out more expensive for the same quoted rate.

Can I prepay and reduce my interest?

Most lenders allow part-prepayment or foreclosure, which can meaningfully cut total interest — ask the lender directly about charges and process once your loan is referred through Andromeda.
