Equity Broking Support

How equity account opening works, step by step

Phoneme Quant is an Authorised Person of Kotak Securities Limited (Kotak Neo) — not the broker itself. Here's exactly what happens, and where, if you open an equity trading account through us.

Start the conversation Read the full disclosure

Tell Phoneme Quant you want to open an account

Reach out by WhatsApp, phone or the contact form and let us know you're looking to trade or invest in equities. We'll explain the Authorised Person model and answer questions before anything else happens.

We introduce you to Kotak Securities (Kotak Neo)

Phoneme Quant acts only as an Authorised Person of Kotak Securities Limited. We point you to the official Kotak Neo account-opening flow — we never ask you to send PAN, Aadhaar or bank details to us directly.

You complete KYC directly with Kotak Securities

Identity verification, bank account linking and the demat/trading account agreement all happen on Kotak Securities' own platform, under their compliance process. This typically involves an in-app or online KYC flow with OTP verification.

Kotak Securities activates your account

Once KYC is verified, Kotak Securities Limited activates your demat and trading account. Orders, execution, funds and securities are always handled by Kotak Securities — never by Phoneme Quant.

You start trading; we stay on for support

You place orders directly on the Kotak Neo platform. If you have questions about the process, need help understanding a statement, or want to raise a service request, Phoneme Quant's team remains your point of contact.

Who does what: Phoneme Quant handles introductions, onboarding support and ongoing service. Kotak Securities Limited handles KYC verification, account activation, order execution, funds and securities custody, and contract notes. Investments in securities market are subject to market risks; read all related documents carefully before investing.

Frequently asked questions

Does Phoneme Quant hold my money or shares?

No. Phoneme Quant does not handle client funds or securities at any point. All funds, securities, orders and contract notes are handled directly by Kotak Securities Limited.

What is an Authorised Person?

An Authorised Person (AP) is a SEBI-recognised category of intermediary appointed by a registered stockbroker to provide services like client acquisition and support on the broker's behalf. Phoneme Quant is an Authorised Person of Kotak Securities Limited, not a broker in its own right.

Will Phoneme Quant recommend which stocks to buy?

No. Phoneme Quant does not provide stock tips, research reports or investment advice. Nothing on this website is a research recommendation.

How long does account opening take?

This depends entirely on Kotak Securities' own KYC and verification process — Phoneme Quant does not control the timeline. Most digital KYC flows are completed within a few business days once documents are submitted correctly.

What if I have an issue after my account is active?

Contact Phoneme Quant first for general service support. Product-specific grievances (like a trade execution dispute) may also be raised directly through Kotak Securities' official grievance process.
