How It Works

What actually happens after you contact us

Phoneme Quant is a distribution and referral business, not the entity that holds your money or issues your policy. Here's exactly where each step happens.

See the detailed equity walkthrough

Tell us what you need

Reach out about equity, mutual funds, insurance or a loan referral — by WhatsApp, phone, or the form below.

We connect you to the regulated principal

Kotak Securities, NJ Wealth, NJ Insurance Brokers or Andromeda — whichever entity actually holds the licence for that product.

You complete KYC directly with them

Account opening, fund transfers and policy issuance all happen on the principal's own platform, under their compliance process — never through Phoneme Quant.

We stay on for ongoing support

Questions after onboarding, service requests, and coordination on your behalf continue through our team.

We don't push products. We connect you to the regulated principal, give you free calculators to run your own numbers, and stay honest about what we do and don't do — no PMS, no advisory, no assured returns. You decide; we make the process and the math easy.
Regulatory Disclosures

Important Information

Each product is facilitated only in the capacity stated below. Product approval, execution, issuance, sanction and servicing remain subject to the relevant regulated principal or lender.

Broking services are provided by Kotak Securities Limited (Kotak Neo). Phoneme Quant acts only in its Authorised Person capacity and does not handle client funds or securities. Investments in securities market are subject to market risks; read all related documents carefully before investing.

Distribution is facilitated through NJ Wealth / NJ India Invest Private Limited, AMFI ARN 352639. Transactions are in Regular Plans where the distributor may receive commission from the relevant AMC. Phoneme Quant does not provide SEBI-registered investment advisory services. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks; read all scheme related documents carefully.

Insurance is the subject matter of solicitation. Life, Non-Life and Health insurance is distributed through NJ Insurance Brokers Private Limited, Insurance Certificate No. POSPN72044. Policy issuance, underwriting and claims are decided solely by the respective insurer.

Loan enquiries are referred through Andromeda Sales and Distribution Private Limited. The relevant bank or NBFC independently decides eligibility, interest rate, sanction and disbursal — Phoneme Quant does not sanction or disburse loans.

Phoneme Quant does not offer portfolio management services (PMS), discretionary portfolio management, stock tips, research reports, investment advisory, algorithmic strategies, technical analysis, commodity strategies or assured/indicative returns. Nothing on this website, including the calculators, is a research recommendation.

Information submitted through this website is used only to respond to your enquiry and coordinate with the applicable service provider after consent. Do not submit PAN, Aadhaar, bank credentials, demat credentials, passwords, PINs or OTPs through the contact form or WhatsApp. Product applications and KYC must be completed only through the official process of the relevant provider.

For a service concern, contact Phoneme Quant first at phonemequant@gmail.com. Product-specific grievances may also be raised through the relevant provider's official grievance process.
